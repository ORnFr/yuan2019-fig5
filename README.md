For practice propose, a reproduction of the figure 5 of the journal article titled 'Theory of variational quantum simulation' published on Quantum, DOI:10.22331/q-2019-10-07-191, is represented here.

This article is mainly about various variational quantum algorithms, which are proposed to simulate the real / imaginary time evolution of the physical systems. The Frenkel-Dirac variational principle, the McLachlan variational principle and the time-dependent variational principle is mainly discussed. The parameterized quantum circuit is introduced to build ansatz, which is conducted by Tensorcircuit. The exact evolution is calculated by means of import QuTiP.
